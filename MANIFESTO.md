## 4. What We Are Building

**NIGHTFALLCOIN** — native coin of the Nightfall chain.

- **Own L1** — consensus, networking, execution, settlement. Not an ERC-20.
- **Privacy by default** — amounts, parties, and graph edges hidden unless you reveal them.
- **Selective disclosure** — you may prove what you choose (view keys, ZK disclosures). Nobody else chooses for you.
- **Censorship resistance** — a chain that can be told what to include is a service, not a protocol.
- **Anti-extraction** — MEV that parasitizes users is treated as a bug in the economic design, not a business model.
- **Rust-core** — memory safety, performance, and code that can be audited without tears.

We are not “faster Ethereum.”  
We are not “Monero with smart contracts cosplay.”  
We are a night settlement layer for humans, agents, and anyone who refuses to live in a glass wallet.
